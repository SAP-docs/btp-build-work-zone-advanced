# Assignment of Content Items

Once you've configured your apps, you need to assign them to various content items so that users can view the apps and access them.

The following assignments must be done before you can view and access your apps:



<a name="loio4d79a087347c4c1abf9483fc6f96bf86__section_gtc_xpq_mgc"/>

## Enable Role-Based Access

Once you've configured your apps, there are 3 levels of role assignments that you need to do in order to access your apps.

> ### Note:  
> An *Everyone* role is provided out-of-the-box for each subaccount. Items assigned to this role are visible to all users in all the sites in the subaccount. The*Everyone* role does not have a corresponding role collection and is assigned to all users implicitly.
