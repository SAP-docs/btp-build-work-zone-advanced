# About Users

SAP Build Work Zone, advanced edition allows access to internal and external users. Internal users are employees of the company while the external users are considered as guests.



<a name="loio4b3d40bf86f24f90afc21713f6d4ab49__section_ehy_nrj_31c"/>

Internal Users

</td>

Company employees who have access to internal information and can collaborate with other employees. They may have different roles in the company such as a company admin or a workspace admin.

</td>

External Users

</td>
<td valign="top">

Guests of the company who are invited to access the company site. They should not be exposed to any internal or confidential information, and therefore have restricted access to content.

</td>
<td valign="top">

[About External Users](about-external-users-4378212.md)

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td valign="top">

Administrators

</td>
<td valign="top">

Employees of the company who manage and configure different settings in the company. The overall administrator is the company admin, but there are also delegated admins such as a support admin or a content admin that have a smaller scope of administrative capabilities.

</td>
<td valign="top">

[About Administrators](about-administrators-63a91fb.md)

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td valign="top">

End Users

</td>
<td valign="top">

Employees of the company who use the company site to perform their daily tasks, however they can't change company settings that affect other users. End users can create and manage workspaces and be workspace administrators.

</td>
<td valign="top">

 

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td valign="top">

Members

</td>
<td valign="top">

Internal or external users who have accepted an invitation to join a workspace, or joined a workspace on their own. To access a private workspace you must be that workspace member.

</td>
<td valign="top" rowspan="2">

[About Members and Non-Members](about-members-and-non-members-0d2d491.md)

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td valign="top">

Non-members

</td>
<td valign="top">

Internal or external users who can access a workspace without joining it. As non-members, they typically have less collaboration options than members and can't perform certain actions that members can. Non-members can only access public workspaces.

</td>
</tr>
