# Assign Apps to a Catalog \(Optional\)

Assign apps to a catalog, to display them in the App Finder in runtime.



> ### Note:  
> Assigning an app to a catalog is optional.



A catalog is a set of apps that are displayed together in the App Finder under the catalog name.

To assign an app to a catalog do the following:

1.  Open the *Content Manager*.

2.  Select the catalog you want to assign your apps to, or create a new catalog by clicking *Create* \> *Catalog*.

3.  If you selected an existing catalog, click *Edit*.
4.  Under the *Apps* tab, you'll see a list of apps that you have permissions to access.

    In the *Assignment Status* column, enable the toggle switch for each app you want to assign to the catalog.

5.  *Save* your changes.
