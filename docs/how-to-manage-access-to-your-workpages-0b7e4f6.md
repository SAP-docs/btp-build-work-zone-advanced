# How to Manage Access to Your Workpages

Learn how you can manage access to the workpages that you create for your workspace.

When you create a workpage in your workspace and add it to the navigation bar, you have the option to manage which users will be able to access the workpage. In addition to appearing in the workspace navigation bar, the workpage is automatically added to the *Content* list of your workspace. The default permission for viewing this workpage in the *Content* list is set to *Hidden*. This means that the workpage is only visible to you the creator of the workpage, and the workspace admin. From the *Content* list, you also have the option to change the permissions for your workpage.

Determine who should be able to view and access a workpage as follows:

1.  Open your workspace displaying your workpage in the navigation bar of the workspace, or click :heavy_plus_sign: to create a new one.

2.  Click the :gear: icon to open the *Manage Tabs* screen.

3.  Next to your workpage, open the dialog box and select *Access Control*.

4.  Select one of the options to determine who can access the workpage and click *Save*.

5.  Now open the *Content* list of your workspace.

    > ### Note:  
    > You can open the *Content* list by clicking the <span class="SAP-icons-V5"></span> icon, and selecting *Content*. You can also add a *Content* tab to the navigation bar of your workspace. In both cases you'll get a list of all content items in your workspace.

    You’ll see your workpage in this list. However, only you as the creator of the workpage and the workspace admin can currently see the workpage because the viewing permissions are set to *Hidden* by default.

6.  To change the default permission, in the *Actions* column of the *Content* list, find the row with your workpage.

7.  Click the <span class="SAP-icons-V5"></span> icon and in the list of options, click *Edit Permissions*.

8.  Select one of the following options to change the permissions:

    -   *Hidden*: in the *Content* list, the workpage is only visible to the creator of the workpage as well as to the workspace admins. This is he default.

    -   *Limited*: in the *Content* list, the workpage can only be edited by the creator of the workpage as well as by the workspace admin.

    -   *Full*: anyone can edit the workpage.


9.  Click *Save*.
