# How to Create Content for Your Workspace

You can create new content for your workspace or you can upload content from your computer and add it to your workspace.



You can create content such as workpages, blog posts, links, videos, wiki pages, documents \(PowerPoint, Word, Excel\), decision-making tools \(such as polls and comparison tables\), as well as planning tools \(such as agenda, tasks, and timelines\) to your workspace.

When you create or upload content for a specific workspace, it's added to the workspace *Content* list.

## How to create content

1.  In your workspace, click the <span class="SAP-icons-V5"></span> \(workspace navigator\) icon.

2.  From the dropdown list, select *Content*.

3.  Click *\+ Create* to create any of the content items in the dropdown list or click *Upload File* to upload content item from your computer .

    > ### Note:  
    > For information about scanning uploaded content for malware, see the section **Frontend Security** in the [Security Guide](security-guide-d7b1a89.md).

4.  -   If you create a new content item, give it a title and publish it. The content item is added to the workspace *Content* list.

-   If you upload a file, define the settings, and click *Upload* to add it to the *Content* list. You can upload a few content items simultaneously.


5.  To see the *Content* list for your workspace, you can either:

    -   Select *Content* from the workspace navigator icon on the right of the navigation bar

    -   Add a *Content* navigation tab to the navigation bar in your workspace by clicking :heavy_plus_sign: on the right of the navigation bar.

    -   Add a *Content* widget to your workpage. In this case you may not see all the content items in the workspace - it depends how many you defined to display in the settings.


    > ### Note:  
    > You can also add navigation tabs in the workspace navigation bar for other types of content to make it easier to access them. Click the :heavy_plus_sign: to add navigation tabs for content such as Workpages, Forums, Events, Members, Feed Updates, and much more.
