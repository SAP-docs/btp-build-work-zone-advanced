# Working with Recent Activities and Frequently Used Apps

Find your most used apps or open a recently used function.

To access Recent Activities and Frequently Used Apps, open the User Actions menu. The Quick Access dialog shows you lists of your most used apps and the last apps you used in the site. Each list displays a maximum of 30 items. You can directly navigate to any of these apps or objects by clicking them.

**Recent Activities**

You see an interactive history of the items you've searched for in Enterprise Search and worked with recently. The apps or objects are listed chronologically.

**Frequently Used Apps**

You see the apps you've searched for and opened using Enterprise Search or the apps used most frequently over the last 30 days. The apps are listed in order of usage frequency with the most used listed first. An app must be used at least twice to appear in the list.
