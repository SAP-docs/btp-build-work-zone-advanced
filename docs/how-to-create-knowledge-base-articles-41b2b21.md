# How to Create Knowledge Base Articles

Learn how to create knowledge base articles to help resolve issues and provide troubleshooting steps.

A knowledge base article is a structured, informative document designed to provide clear and concise answers to common questions, problems, or topics related to a specific subject. These articles are typically part of a knowledge base - a centralized repository of information that helps users \(customers, employees, or stakeholders\) find solutions independently.



## Create a knowledge base article

To create a knowledge base article, do the following:

1.  From your workspace navigation bar, click :heavy_plus_sign: and select the *Knowledge Base* tile. This adds the Knowledge Base tab to the workspace navigation bar.
2.  Click *\+ Knowldege Base Article* and select one of the templates that suits your use case. Your options are:
    -   *Topic Page*: Enter a title, a brief overview of the topic, the details, any additional information, and attachments.

    -   *How-to Article*: Enter a title, brief description, step-by-step instructions, and additional information, and attachments.

    -   *Frequently Asked Questions*: Enter a title and up to 10 questions with answers.

    -   *Problem Resoltion*: Enter symptoms, the environment, causes, resolution, issue type, and select an impact level.


3.  Click *Create*.
4.  Enter a title in the *Title* box.

5.  Fill in the remaining text boxes for the chosen template as needed using the rich text editor and add any relevant content such as images.

6.  To add an attachment, click *Add Attachment* at the bottom of the article. You can attach existing content from the workspace or upload a new file. While you can't attach a mirrored item to your article, you can insert a link to it.

7.  To the left of your article, you can choose applicable categories, making it easier for others to find it when they do a search. Categories can be selected before or after you create the article. Once published, the list of categories that the article belongs to displays on the right side of the article in single item view. Your company administrator is responsible for creating the categories.

8.  Once your article is complete, click *Publish*. You can choose the publishing and expiration dates for your article to help ensure timeliness and relevance of the article. And you can also set workspace member permissions for knowledge base article access: Full, Limited, or Hidden access to control view and edit capabilities. You can also save your article as a draft and finish it later.

    > ### Note:  
    > Depending on the configuration by the workspace administrator, the article may be subject to a content approval process. You'll receive a notification when your article is approved or rejected.

9.  If you want the article to generate a feed item once published, choose *Major change* and enter a comment about the article or version.



## Copy a knowledge base article

You can copy a knowledge base article within the same workspace or another workspace you are a member of, to create new articles similar to the existing ones.

To copy an article, do the following:

1.  From the list of knowledge base articles on your workspace Knowledge Base page, click the drop-down on the far right of the article you want to copy and choose *Copy*.

2.  Click *OK* in the confirmation dialog to proceed with the copy action.




## Use thumbnails

You can include a thumbnail image with your articles to help users visually identify content. They are included when knowledge base articles are copied, moved, or mirrored. If your workspace content approval is set up for knowledge base articles, any changes to the thumbnail image will also be subject to the content approval workflow.

For more information, see [How to Add Thumbnails to Your Content](how-to-add-thumbnails-to-your-content-0174ab7.md).



## Edit or delete a knowledge base article

Workspace administrators and knowledge base article authors can edit and delete published knowledge base articles. After editing, articles are submitted for approval through the content approval process, if configured. The published article remains available to workspace members and displays the updated version once changes are approved.

If the workspace collaboration level is set to *Full* and the knowledge base article permission is also set to*Full*, members of the workspace can delete an already published knowledge base article.

To edit an article, go to the navigation tab in your workspace and click on *Knowledge Base*. This displays a list of all available knowledge base articles for the workspace. To edit, either select *Edit* from the drop-down menu or click the article to open it in single item view and then click *Edit*.

Don't forget to publish your changes. You can also delete the article by selecting *Delete* from the drop-down list.



## Use more features

-   Use the *Summarize*button to enable AI input.

-   Revert to a previous version of the KBA. When you publish an article, it receives a version number. Each time the article is republished, the version number increases by one. The latest version of the article always appears in the knowledge base widget.

-   Search for KBAs in private and public workspaces that you have access to using keywords. External users can also search for knowledge base articles in worskpaces they have access to.

-   You can also recommend knowledge base articles as well as submit feedback about them.
