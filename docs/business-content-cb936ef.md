# Business Content

You can add and configure business content in the *Content Manager*.



In the Site Manager, Content Manager screen, you manage business content that is available in your subaccount. Business content, or design-time content, consists of applications, roles, catalogs, groups, spaces and pages. Complying with the same common data model, content from SAP and non-SAP content providers can be integrated in a unified manner in the site.

To access the Content Manager, in the *Administration Console*, go to *External Integrations* \> *Business Content*.

## Procedure

1.  Add the business content to your subaccount level. To do that, you need to go through a configuration procedure that includes creating content providers, configuring destinations, assigning apps to your site and more. For more information, see [Integrating Business Content](integrating-business-content-b7fa275.md).
2.  After you've assigned the applications to your site, you can:
    -   Add the predefined *Applications* page to the site menu. There, all the apps that are assigned to the site are displayed as tiles. The *Applications* page is filtered according to roles, therefore users can only see the apps assigned to them.

    -   Add apps directly to a workpage. For more information, see [How to Add Applications to Your Workpage](how-to-add-applications-to-your-workpage-7bcef04.md).


**App Finder**:

You can find all the apps that you have permission to access in a tool called the App Finder. The App Finder contains apps arranged in catalogs and you can assign apps to your site directly from the App Finder. To access the App Finder, click the <span class="SAP-icons-V5"></span> icon from the header of your screen or from the User Actions menu, depending on how you defined it in the *Site Settings* screen.

> ### Note:  
> When viewing the site applications from the SAP menu on the top-left side of your site, you will also see there system apps such as Calender, Notification, etc. These apps are not considered as business content and therefore are not visible in the Content Manager.
