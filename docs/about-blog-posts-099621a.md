# About Blog Posts

You can write a blog post to share ideas and thoughts.

Your followers or fellow workspace members can provide feedback on your blog post by commenting on it.

To see all the blog posts in a workspace, navigate to the *Content* list and select *Blog Posts*.

> ### Note:  
> Blog posts can only be modified by the blog post's creator or a workspace administrator.

For more information about how to create a blog post or how to create a blog on behalf of another author, see [How to Create a Blog](how-to-create-a-blog-20f013a.md).
