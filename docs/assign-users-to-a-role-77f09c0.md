# Assign Users to a Role

In order for users to access their apps, they must be assigned to the same role that the app is assigned to.

Once you create a local role in the Content Manager, a corresponding role collection is created in the SAP BTP cockpit. To assign this role collection to users, do the following:

1.  In the side menu of the SAP BTP cockpit, go to *Security* \> *Role Collections*.

2.  Search for the relevant role collection.

3.  Open the role collection for editing. Click the arrow at the far right, and in the role collection screen, click*Edit*.

4.  In the *Users* section of the screen, search for your email and add it to the list of users by clicking the *\+* button.

5.  Click *Save*.


> ### Note:  
> Alternatively, you can perform the same admin role assignment in the *Security* \> *Users* screen, by first searching for your email and then assigning your email to the role collection.
