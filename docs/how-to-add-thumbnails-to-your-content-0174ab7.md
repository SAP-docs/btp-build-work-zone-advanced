# How to Add Thumbnails to Your Content

Thumbnail images provide a way to show a visual representation of wiki pages, blog posts, knowledge base articles \(KBAs\), workpages, and videos so that users can quickly identify them.

When wikis, blog posts, KBAs, and videos are created, a default thumbnail image is auto generated. Anyone who has permission to edit these content items can customize the thumbnail too.

To edit a wiki page, blog post, KBA, or workpage thumbnail image, do the following:

1.  Go to the specific content item's view \(in other words to the screen where you create these content items\).

2.  In the top left corner, click the :camera: icon to open the *Edit Thumbnail*dialog box.

3.  Select an image from your workspace *Content* folder or upload a new image.

4.  From the *Thumbnail* box, you can rotate your image using the dedicated buttons and you can also move the image up and down to fit the thumbnail dimensions \(1:1 ratio\).

5.  Click *Save*.

*Videos*

> ### Note:  
> When you create or upload a video, you can customize the thumbnail image that appears when the video displays in widgets, playlists, views, feed posts, or in the mobile app.

To select a video thumbnail image, do the following:

1.  Open your video.

2.  Select *Video Manager* \> *Thumbnails*.
3.  Select one of the five thumbnails that are automatically chosen from frames within your video.

    You can also upload a new image and then crop, zoom, or pan the image if necessary.

4.  Click *Save*.

The video thumbnail is bundled with the video.

For more information, see [How to Record and Enhance Your Videos](how-to-record-and-enhance-your-videos-61c6389.md) and review the *Using Thumbnail Images* section.

> ### Note:  
> Thumbnail images used for wikis, blogs, workpages, or KBAs can be adjusted using a slider or dragging an image so that it’s displayed properly in a *Content* widget with different layouts \(thumbnail/gallery/carousel\) and in the *Content* list in grid view.
