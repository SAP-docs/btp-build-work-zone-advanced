# Assign Apps to Groups 

Assign apps to a group, to display them in the runtime site.



A group is a set of apps that are displayed together in the site under the group name. To be visible in the site, an app must be assigned to a group.

To assign an app to a group, do the following:

1.  Open the *Content Manager*.

2.  Select the group you want to assign your apps to, or create a new group by clicking *Create* \> *Group*.

3.  If you selected an existing group, click *Edit*.
4.  Under the *Apps* tab in the group editor, you'll see a list of apps that you have permissions to access.

    In the *Assignment Status* column, enable the toggle switch for each app you want to assign to the group.

5.  *Save* your changes.


> ### Note:  
> Group order is automatically sorted alphabetically, and is not case sensitive.
> 
> The order logic may be different from groups coming from on-premise launchpads.
> 
> End users can personalize the order of their groups if this setting has been enabled by the administrator.

> ### Note:  
> Click an item to view its details before assigning or unassigning it.
