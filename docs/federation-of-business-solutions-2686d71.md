# Federation of Business Solutions

Learn about the consumption options of HTML5 business solutions and Local-Entry-Point business solutions.

## What is a Business Solution?

A business solution is typically a multitarget application that consists of UI modules, business logic and a connection to a database. A business solution also contains a CDM file with all the design time definitions, making the solution ready to be consumed with no or minimal admin-side configuration.

There are two types of business solutions: HTML5 business solution that is consumed as a content provider and requires a subscription to SAP Build Work Zone, and a Local-Entry-Point business solution, that is available only in SAP Build Work Zone, standard edition.

To learn more about the different types of business solutions and their advantages compared to the legacy HTML5 apps, see [Comparing Development Scenarios](comparing-development-scenarios-be2d5ca.md).
