## How to create a blog

1.  Click *Create* \> *Blog Post* from the *Content* list of your workspace.

2.  In the blog editor that opens, enter a title and your text.

3.  Save your blog as a draft to proofread or edit it and then publish.


> ### Note:  
> When you save a blog post as a draft, the draft is only visible to you and other people can't access it. If you want to make your draft accessible to others, you need to publish it. If you click to edit a blog while someone else is working on it, you need to choose to continue or cancel. If you continue, a new draft of the blog is created.

> ### Note:  
> > ### Note:  
> > Once you've created your blog, a default thumbnail image is auto generated. A thumbnail image enables you to personalize your blog so that others can quickly identify them. For more information about how to use thumbnails, see [How to Add Thumbnails to Your Content](how-to-add-thumbnails-to-your-content-0174ab7.md).

## Blog on Behalf of

You can designate a blog post author, which allows other people to publish and edit blogs on your behalf.

**Prerequisites**

The company administrator, has enabled the feature *Designate Blog Post Author* in the *Administration Console* \> *Features* \> *Feature Management*

**How to designate a blog post author**

As a user, you can set other people as blog post authors.

1.  From the User Action menu, choose Settings*Settings* \> *Advanced Settings* \> *Designate Blog Post Author* and click *Save*.

2.  On the *Designate Blog Post Author* screen, enter a name or an e-mail address in the search bar. Select the suggested person from the list.

3.  Your designated blog post authors are displayed in a list below the search bar. If you want to remove a person from the list, choose the x next to the person's name.

4.  Choose the author as follows:

    1.  Create a blog post.

    2.  Choose Publish.

    3.  In the *Publish* window, from the *Publish* dropdown menu, select an author.

        By default, your name is listed as the first option.

    4.  Choose *Publish*.



For more information about publishing settings, see [Publish Settings](publish-settings-b69d86a.md).
