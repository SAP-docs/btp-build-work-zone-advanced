# How to Create a Filter for Your Company Feed

Create your own filters for your Company feed.

There are various default filters that you can use to filter your company feed.

For more information, see [About Feeds](about-feeds-ce48b2a.md) 

However, you can also create your own filter as follows:

1.  At the top of your company feed, from the dropdown menu, choose *Show More*.

2.  Select *Manage Filters*.

3.  Choose *\+New Filter*.

4.  Select the filter type - filter specified members or workspaces.

5.  Click *Submit*.
