# About Spaces and Pages

In SAP Build Work Zone, standard edition, spaces and pages provide a structured way to organize and access applications.



<a name="loio7883d4782b4e4c43b077f5df50632085__section_gx5_4x4_vfc"/>

## What is a space and what is a page?

A **space** is a container that groups related content and it has one or more pages assigned to it. You create a space in the Space editor located in the Content Manager. Spaces are assigned to a role to enable role-based access to them and users assigned to the same role are able to access the space and see the relevant pages assigned to it. Spaces are located in the menu bar of your runtime page.

A **page** is where you add your apps and content. You can create pages in the Page editor located in the *Content Manager* - these pages are referred to as local pages. Pages must be assigned to a space so that users can see and access the pages with its apps and content . Pages can be further categorized into sections for related content.

## About spaces and pages in different view modes

In SAP Build Work Zone, standard edition, you can select which view mode to work in depending on how the content is modeled \(groups or spaces and pages\). For more information about the different view modes, see [Site Settings](site-settings-ca74965.md) under *Display Settings* \> *View Mode.*

You can use spaces and pages in one of the following view modes:

-   In *Spaces and Pages* view mode, you'll see only spaces and pages that are federated from remote content providers. The content is read-only.

-   In the *Spaces and Pages - New Experience* view mode, you'll see spaces and pages that are locally created in the Page editor side by side with the federated pages from remote content providers.

    > ### Note:  
    > You can view your local content that you created in *Spaces and Pages - New Experience* mode, from the *Applications* page in the site menu.

    > ### Note:  
    > When switching the view mode to *Spaces and Pages – New Experience* in the *Site Settings* screen, it may take a while before you can see the updated content.

## Things to know about spaces and pages

Here are some facts about spaces and pages that you should know:

-   Spaces and pages are assigned to users through their business roles, ensuring that users only see the content relevant to their specific work. Therefore you need to also:
    -   Assign spaces to your roles so that you can access them. You can do this in the Role editor that you access from the *Content Manager*.

    -   Assign your pages to a space. This makes your pages visible in the spaces you manually created. You can do this in the Space editor from the *Pages* tab that you can access from the *Content Manager*.


-   At runtime, the navigation bar above the page shows the space name. If you have several spaces assigned you can click on the arrow next to a space to navigate to the page that belongs to the space.
