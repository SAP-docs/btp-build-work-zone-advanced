# Business Record Widget

Use this widget to link to specific business records and feature them in your workspace.

If SAP Build Work Zone, advanced edition has been integrated with external business systems such as SAP Cloud for Customer, by adding a business record widget to your workpage you can display key details from a specific business record \(for example, a sales opportunity or a service ticket\) thus providing visibility and context to users within a workspace.

You can set the following details for the business record:

-   *Show:* From the dropdown list, you can select Item Detail, Related, Featured, or Specific records.

    > ### Note:  
    > You can select specific business records within the company or workspace and add them to your workpages.

-   *Type:* If you select a *Featured* Business Record, you can select which type you want to display. Options are Corporate Accounts , Tasks, Opportunities, and Service Requests.

-   *Layout:* Options are List and Card.

-   Use the slider to select how many records you want to display.

-   Save your settings.
