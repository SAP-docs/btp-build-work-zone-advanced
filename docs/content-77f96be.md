# Content

Besides workpages, there's a wide variety of content that you can add to the workpages of your workspace.



Some of the content involves adding widgets and cards from a content finder, while some content like blogs or wikis that you or a member of the workspace has created or uploaded from a computer. Use the following how to guides to help you add content to your workpages:
